title: "Customers"
description: "Create individual or business customers before enabling financial capabilities."
---

Create the customer that will own capabilities, accounts, and money movement in your integration.

## Individual customers

Create an individual customer and store the returned customer ID. Use your own stable external identifier to connect the customer to your system.

## Business customers

Create a business customer and store its ID. Add related parties when the business onboarding flow requires them.

## Next step

Use the customer ID to [enable a capability and complete its requirements](/integration/onboarding/capabilities-and-requirements).

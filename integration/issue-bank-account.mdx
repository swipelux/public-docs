title: "Issue a bank account"
description: "Create a settlement wallet, issue a bank account, and monitor provisioning."
---

Issue reusable bank details for a customer after the relevant capability is ready.

## Use a settlement wallet

Create or select the issued wallet that will receive funds from the bank account. Keep its account ID for the bank-account request.

## Monitor provisioning

Create the issued bank account, inspect its current status, and wait for provisioning before presenting bank details. Routing details may be absent until the account is ready.

## Next step

Review [Accounts and wallets](/integration/accounts), then use [Sync and reconciliation](/integration/sync-and-reconciliation) to keep account state current.

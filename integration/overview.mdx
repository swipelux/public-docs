## How an integration works

1. **Create a customer.** Create the person or business that owns the flow with [`POST /v3/customers`](/api-reference/customers/post-v3-customers).
2. **Choose the intended outcome.** Decide whether the customer needs a pay-in, payout, or issued bank account.
3. **Request an eligible capability.** Discover the customer's options with [`GET /v3/customers/{customerId}/capabilities/supported`](/api-reference/capabilities/get-v3-customers-by-customer-id-capabilities-supported), then request the option that matches the outcome.
4. **Complete current requirements.** Read open tasks and provide the requested hosted or API responses.
5. **Create the account or destination.** Prepare the customer-owned account or third-party destination needed by the selected flow.
6. **Run and monitor the outcome.** For money movement, quote, execute, and monitor the transfer. For an issued bank account, create the account and monitor provisioning.

## Core resources

- **Customer.** The individual or business that owns capabilities, accounts, and transfers.
- **Capability.** A customer-specific permission and onboarding lifecycle for a payment method and direction.
- **Account.** A customer-owned issued or external wallet or bank account, created with [`POST /v3/customers/{customerId}/accounts`](/api-reference/accounts/post-v3-customers-by-customer-id-accounts).
- **Recipient.** A person or business that receives funds from the customer, created with [`POST /v3/customers/{customerId}/recipients`](/api-reference/recipients/post-v3-customers-by-customer-id-recipients).
- **Destination.** A recipient's bank or wallet endpoint, created with [`POST /v3/customers/{customerId}/recipients/{recipientId}/destinations`](/api-reference/recipients/post-v3-customers-by-customer-id-recipients-by-recipient-id-destinations).
- **Quote.** A time-bound price for a specific source, destination, and amount.
- **Transfer.** The execution and current state of an accepted quote.

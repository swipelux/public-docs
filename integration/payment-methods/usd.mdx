A USD bank account gives your customer reusable US bank details for ACH, wire, and SWIFT deposits. Deposits are credited to the customer's USDC or USDT settlement wallet. Payouts in USD go out over the same three rails.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Currency | USD |
| Methods | ACH, wire, SWIFT |
| Named capabilities | `ach_named`, `wire_named`, `swift_named` |
| Pooled capabilities | `ach_pooled`, `wire_pooled`, `swift_pooled` |
| Receive | Named ACH, wire, and SWIFT accounts. Pooled ACH and wire pay-ins. |
| Send | ACH, wire, and SWIFT payouts |

Pooled SWIFT does not receive. To receive SWIFT deposits, issue a named SWIFT account.

## Request the capability

Read the customer's supported capabilities and request the variant you need. The response lists the banks open to this customer and marks the default with `isDefault`, which can differ between customers. In the sandbox, ACH, wire, and SWIFT are served only at JPMorgan Chase (`jpmorgan`). See [Capabilities and requirements](/integration/onboarding/capabilities-and-requirements#discover-supported-capabilities) and [Test in the sandbox](/integration/sandbox).

## Receive

- **Reusable details.** Create an issued account with `method` set to `ach`, `wire`, or `swift`, `country` set to `US`, and `currency` set to `USD`. See [Issue a bank account](/integration/issue-bank-account).
- **One-time pay-in.** Quote a pay-in on a pooled or named ACH or wire capability and show the transfer's funding instructions. Pooled wire pay-ins need at least 20.00 USD. Below a route's minimum, `POST /v3/quotes` returns `422 amount_not_deliverable`. See [Receive funds](/integration/receive-funds).

### Account details

An ACH or wire account returns `routingNumber`, `accountNumber`, `accountType`, `bankName`, `accountHolderName`, and `bankAddress`. It adds `achRoutingNumber` and `wireRoutingNumber` when the bank supplies separate numbers. When `referenceRequired` is `true`, show the exact `reference`.

A SWIFT account returns `bankName`, `swiftCode`, `accountNumber`, `accountHolderName`, `accountHolderAddress`, `bankAddress`, and any intermediary bank fields. Senders must copy the beneficiary address exactly.

When a routing number, account number, or SWIFT code changes, Swipelux sends `account.details_changed`. Refetch the account and show the new details.

## Send

Pay a customer-owned [external account](/integration/accounts) or a [recipient destination](/integration/recipients). An `ach` or `wire` destination needs `routingNumber`, `accountNumber`, `bankName`, `accountHolderName`, and `country`. `accountType` is `checking` or `savings`.

Some banks require a `purpose` on ACH and wire payout quotes. See [Declare a payout purpose](/integration/quotes-and-transfers#declare-a-payout-purpose).

### SWIFT payouts

USD SWIFT payouts need a ready `swift_pooled` or `swift_named` capability. Fund them with USDC on Ethereum, Base, or Polygon, or with USDT on Ethereum or Polygon. The recipient amount after fees must be at least 100.00 USD.

A SWIFT destination needs the bank's street address in `bankAddress`, `bankCity`, `bankState`, and `bankPostalCode`. The BIC country must match the bank country. Swipelux takes the beneficiary address from the customer or recipient profile. For a payout to a third party, the bank can ask for a supporting document, such as an invoice. See [Send funds](/integration/send-funds#swift-payouts) and [Supporting documents for SWIFT payouts](/integration/quotes-and-transfers#supporting-documents-for-swift-payouts).

For SWIFT in other currencies, see [Payment methods](/integration/payment-methods/overview#other-currencies-over-swift).

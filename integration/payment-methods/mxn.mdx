An MXN account gives your customer a CLABE that receives SPEI transfers. Deposits are credited to the customer's USDC settlement wallet. A SPEI recipient destination pays a CLABE.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Currency | MXN |
| Method | SPEI (`spei`) |
| Capability | `spei_pooled` |
| Receive | Issued accounts at a bank from the `banks` list |
| Send | SPEI destinations. Check `directions` on `spei_pooled`. |

## Request the capability

On `spei_pooled`, the `banks` list names the banks that open MXN accounts. Take the account's `institution` from `banks`, not from `institutions`, which can also include banks that serve quotes only. See [Check where an account can open](/integration/onboarding/capabilities-and-requirements#check-where-an-account-can-open).

## Receive

Create an issued account with `method` set to `spei` and `currency` set to `MXN`, and name the `institution`. See [Choose the bank](/integration/issue-bank-account#choose-the-bank).

### Account details

The account returns `details.clabe`. See [Present bank details safely](/integration/issue-bank-account#4-present-bank-details-safely).

## Send

A SPEI [recipient destination](/integration/recipients) needs `clabe` and `accountHolderName`. `bankName`, `institutionName`, and `country` are optional. Before you quote an MXN payout, check that the customer's `spei_pooled` entry lists `payout` in `directions`. See [`POST /v3/customers/{customerId}/recipients/{recipientId}/destinations`](/api-reference/recipients/post-v3-customers-by-customer-id-recipients-by-recipient-id-destinations) for the field formats.

MXN can also go out over SWIFT with a Lead Bank `swift_named` capability, to an 18-digit CLABE or an IBAN. See [Foreign-currency SWIFT payouts](/integration/send-funds#foreign-currency-swift-payouts).

Next, request `spei_pooled` through [Capabilities and requirements](/integration/onboarding/capabilities-and-requirements#request-the-capability), then [issue an MXN bank account](/integration/issue-bank-account).

Each guide here covers one currency. It lists the capability IDs to request, whether the customer gets named or pooled bank details, what the account details contain, and what a payout destination needs. For the full steps, it links to the integration guides.

## Named and pooled

A capability ID ends in `_named` or `_pooled`:

- **Named**: dedicated, reusable bank details for the customer, which you [issue as a bank account](/integration/issue-bank-account).
- **Pooled**: a shared Swipelux bank account. Each pay-in uses a unique reference to route the funds.

Some pooled capabilities also issue accounts: `pix_pooled` for customers not resident in Brazil, and `npp_pooled`, `spei_pooled`, and `nip_pooled` at a bank from the `banks` list. See [Pooled vs named account types](/integration/onboarding/capabilities-and-requirements#pooled-vs-named-account-types) and [Check where an account can open](/integration/onboarding/capabilities-and-requirements#check-where-an-account-can-open).

## Rules for every method

- **Bank choice.** The supported-capability response lists the banks open to each customer. It is not a free choice of any bank.
- **Settlement.** Deposits to an issued bank account are credited to the customer's USDC or USDT settlement wallet. USDT needs a USD account. See [Choose the settlement currency](/integration/issue-bank-account#choose-the-settlement-currency).
- **Payout purpose.** Some banks require a `purpose` on ACH, wire, AUD (NPP), and AED (IPP) payout quotes. See [Declare a payout purpose](/integration/quotes-and-transfers#declare-a-payout-purpose).
- **Timing.** Swipelux does not publish one fixed settlement time for every pay-in capability. Track each transfer to a final state. See [Settlement timing](/knowledge-base/compliance/settlement-slas).
- **Limits.** Limits depend on the customer type, verification level, and risk review. See [Transaction limits](/knowledge-base/compliance/transaction-limits).

## Other currencies over SWIFT

With a ready Lead Bank (`lead_bank`) `swift_named` capability, a customer can open SWIFT accounts and send SWIFT payouts in AUD, CAD, CHF, CZK, DKK, EUR, GBP, HKD, HUF, JPY, MXN, NOK, NZD, PLN, SEK, SGD, and ZAR. BMO Bank N.A. (`bmo`) is the bank reported on these accounts. The bank decides which currencies each customer can use. See [Foreign-currency SWIFT payouts](/integration/send-funds#foreign-currency-swift-payouts).

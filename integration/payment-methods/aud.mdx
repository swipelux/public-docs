An AUD account gives your customer a BSB and account number that receive NPP payments from the customer's own bank account. Deposits are credited to the customer's USDC settlement wallet. AUD payouts go out over NPP.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Currency | AUD |
| Method | NPP (`npp`) |
| Capability | `npp_pooled` |
| Bank | BC Payments Australia Pty Ltd |
| Receive | Issued accounts at a bank from the `banks` list |
| Send | NPP payouts on `npp_pooled` |
| Who can pay in | Only the customer's own bank account |

## Request the capability

On `npp_pooled`, the `banks` list names the banks that open AUD accounts. Take the account's `institution` from `banks`, not from `institutions`, which can also include banks that serve quotes only. See [Check where an account can open](/integration/onboarding/capabilities-and-requirements#check-where-an-account-can-open).

AUD accounts are held at BC Payments Australia Pty Ltd, and AUD payouts go through the same bank. It does not open accounts for businesses registered in, or individuals resident in, Algeria (`DZ`), Bangladesh (`BD`), Egypt (`EG`), India (`IN`), Indonesia (`ID`), Kenya (`KE`), Morocco (`MA`), Nigeria (`NG`), or Pakistan (`PK`).

## Receive

Create an issued account with `method` set to `npp` and `currency` set to `AUD`, and name the `institution`. See [Choose the bank](/integration/issue-bank-account#choose-the-bank).

The account accepts pay-ins only from the customer's own bank account (`payin.senders: "account_holder"`). A payment from anyone else is held for a `transaction_rfi` task, then credited or returned.

### Account details

The account returns `details.bsb` and `details.accountNumber`. See [Present bank details safely](/integration/issue-bank-account#4-present-bank-details-safely).

## Send

An NPP [recipient destination](/integration/recipients) pays an Australian bank account in AUD. Send the 6-digit `bsb` and an `accountNumber` of 5 to 9 digits. Swipelux removes spaces and hyphens before it validates them. `country` defaults to `AU`, and any currency other than AUD returns `400 validation_error`.

Payouts need the customer's ready `npp_pooled` capability. Some banks require a `purpose` on AUD payout quotes. See [Declare a payout purpose](/integration/quotes-and-transfers#declare-a-payout-purpose).

Next, request `npp_pooled` through [Capabilities and requirements](/integration/onboarding/capabilities-and-requirements#request-the-capability), then [issue an AUD bank account](/integration/issue-bank-account).

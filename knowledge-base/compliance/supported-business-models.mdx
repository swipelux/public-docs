## Supported Verticals

Swipelux classifies business models into three risk tiers.

### OK/Allowed

- **E-commerce & SaaS:** Payments for goods/services
- **Web3 Infrastructure:** Non-custodial UI
- **Other instruments that leverage blockchain technology on the UX/UI level**

### Conditional (Enhanced Due Diligence)

- **Licensed Gambling:** Must hold valid license (e.g. local authorizations on the target markets)
- **Trading/CFD:** Must hold a financial investment license
- **Wallet Orchestrators:** Must prove non-custodial, UX/UI-only status

### Prohibited (Rejected)

- **Unlicensed Gambling:** Any wagering without license
- **Adult Content:** Pornography or sexually explicit services
- **Privacy Coins/Mixers:** Monero, Tornado Cash, or obfuscation tools
- **Shell Entities:** Entities with no physical presence
- **Sanctioned jurisdictions:** Material exposure to sanctioned countries, regions and individuals

### Verticals not listed

Verticals that do not appear in the tiers above, such as pharmacies, healthcare, or general retail, have no fixed classification. Swipelux classifies them during the KYB review based on the declared business activity. To confirm a classification before you apply, contact the compliance team.

## High-risk activity declarations

During KYB, the business declares whether it engages in any of the following activities:

- Marijuana or related services
- Adult entertainment
- Gambling
- Holding client funds
- Investment services
- Lending or banking
- Money services
- Foreign exchange or virtual currency brokerage or OTC trading
- Safe deposit box rentals
- Third-party payment processing
- Weapons, firearms, and explosives

Declared activities feed the policy review together with the risk tiers above and can require [enhanced due diligence](/knowledge-base/business-onboarding/kyb-workflow#enhanced-due-diligence-edd-triggers).

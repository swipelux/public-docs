## Jurisdiction Framework

Swipelux has no single list of supported countries. Each Bank sets the countries it accepts for the accounts it holds, and its list applies only to accounts at that Bank.

### Whose country counts

- **Business:** the country where the business is registered.
- **Individual:** the country where the person lives.
- **Lead Bank** checks every country on the customer's profile, and accepts the customer only when it accepts all of them.

US customers are accepted. Businesses registered in the US and individuals who live there can open USD accounts at JPMorgan Chase, CFSB and Lead Bank, and US businesses can open pooled accounts at Citi.

When a Bank does not accept a customer's country, the customer's supported capabilities list that Bank as unavailable with the reason `jurisdiction_unsupported`. See [Show why a bank is unavailable](/integration/onboarding/capabilities-and-requirements#show-why-a-bank-is-unavailable). For identity checks, see [individual onboarding](/integration/onboarding/customers#individual-customers).

### Payout destinations

Payouts have no country list. The account format of each method sets where the money can go:

| Payout | Destination |
| --- | --- |
| AED | A UAE IBAN |
| NGN | A bank account in Nigeria |
| GBP | A UK account with a sort code |
| AUD | An Australian account with a BSB |
| EUR | Any IBAN that receives EUR |
| USD by SWIFT | A bank in any country except the US. Pay a US bank by Wire |

See [send funds](/integration/send-funds) for what each destination needs.

### Cards and crypto

- **Card checkout** (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Cash App, SEPA and BLIK): Swipelux applies no country list. Card pay-ins are in EUR or USD. See [receive funds](/integration/receive-funds).
- **Crypto deposits and payouts:** Swipelux applies no country list.

### Screening

Country lists are not the only check. Swipelux screens customers against sanctions and PEP lists, and screens wallets and transactions. See [Screening and Monitoring](/knowledge-base/compliance/screening-and-monitoring).

### Disclaimer

This page is a high-level overview of the country rules in the product and is not exhaustive.

Swipelux's ability to provide services in a specific country depends on each Bank's policy, on sanctions and AML/CTF obligations, and on its own risk review.

Because Bank policies and regulations change, the countries and rules on this page may change at any time.

Swipelux and each Bank may accept or decline any customer or end-user, or restrict a payment method, based on regulatory, technical, or risk-based considerations, whether or not the country appears on this page.

Final onboarding decisions are made at Swipelux's and the Bank's sole discretion, in line with applicable law.

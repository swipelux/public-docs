## General Information

Swipelux Inc is a technology company.

Swipelux provides the wallet infrastructure in the flow, including wallet creation, crypto custody, and blockchain execution. The exchange of virtual assets is performed by licensed third-party providers in the relevant country, in accordance with local law.

Sub-merchants do not perform VASP-regulated activity in these flows. They:

- control UX/UI and user acquisition
- connect users to Swipelux payment infrastructure
- never hold, control, or access private keys
- never custody funds or execute blockchain transactions

### Regulatory Perimeter

Sub-merchants remain responsible for determining whether their own activities require a license or registration in any jurisdiction, including local sector licensing such as gambling or CFD licensing.

### Compliance Responsibilities

- **Swipelux:** KYC, sanctions, PEP screening, wallet screening, blockchain execution
- **Sub-merchant:** marketing compliance, sector-specific licensing

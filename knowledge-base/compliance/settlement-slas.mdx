## Settlement SLAs

Swipelux settles funds as stablecoins immediately after the incoming payment is confirmed on the underlying payment rail. The effective settlement SLA for your customer is therefore determined by the payment method they use. Once funds arrive, Swipelux processing takes only minutes.

### SLA by payment method

| Payment method | Rail identifier | Settlement SLA | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RTP | `rtp` | Near-instant | US real-time payments. Processed within minutes of receipt. |
| SEPA Instant | `sepa_instant` | Near-instant | Euro-area instant credit transfer. |
| PIX | `pix` | Near-instant | Brazil. Processed within minutes of receipt. |
| Faster Payments | `faster_payments` | Near-instant | UK real-time payments. |
| UAEFTS | `uaefts` | Near-instant | UAE Funds Transfer System. |
| SPEI | `spei` | Near-instant | Mexico domestic transfers. |
| PSE | `pse` | Same business day | Colombia bank debit. |
| Transferências 3.0 | `transfers_3_0` | Same business day | Brazil bank transfer. |
| ACH | `ach` | T+1 | Settled after bank confirmation. |
| Wire (Fedwire) | `wire` | T+2 | Typically same or next business day; T+2 as maximum threshold. |
| SEPA | `sepa` | T+1 | Standard SEPA credit transfer timing. |

Rail identifiers match the values accepted by the Swipelux API when creating a pay-in.

### How it works

1. Your customer sends fiat via their chosen payment method.
2. The underlying rail settles the funds to the designated bank account.
3. On bank confirmation, Swipelux converts and settles stablecoins to the specified wallet — or holds them on the customer's balance — within minutes.

### Recommendation

Communicate the maximum SLA to your merchants, for example "Wire: up to 2 business days". Funds arriving earlier will always settle earlier. The SLA is an upper bound, not a fixed delay.

For the full list of supported rails and how to select one, see [payment methods](/knowledge-base/compliance/payment-methods). For pay-in implementation guidance, see [receive funds](/integration/receive-funds).

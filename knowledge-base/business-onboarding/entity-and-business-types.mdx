## Entity and business classification concepts

Swipelux uses the following concepts to classify a business during KYB review.

<Warning>
  These labels are policy classification concepts. They are not current request
  enums, and they do not guarantee that a business is eligible or approved.
</Warning>

## Business entity types

| Entity type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Limited Liability Company | LLC or equivalent |
| Corporation | General corporation |
| S Corporation | US S corporation |
| C Corporation | US C corporation |
| Partnership | General partnership |
| Limited Partnership | Limited partnership |
| Sole Proprietorship | Individually owned business |
| Nonprofit | Nonprofit organization |
| Trust | Business trust |
| Cooperative | Member-owned cooperative |
| DAO | Decentralized autonomous organization |
| Foundation | Foundation entity |
| Other | Another entity form |
